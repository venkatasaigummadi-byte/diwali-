Safety First

Celebrate Diwali with Safety & Joy

Fireworks bring supreme joy when handled responsibly. Please follow these essential precautions for a safe celebration.

✅ DO's for Safe Fireworks

  • 💧 Always keep two buckets of water or sand nearby for emergency extinguishing.
  • 👕 Wear snug cotton clothes and footwear while bursting firecrackers.
  • 📏 Light fireworks outdoors in an open space, away from dried leaves and wires.
  • 🕯️ Always use an incense stick (agarbatti) or long sparkler to ignite crackers at arm's length.
  • 👨‍👧 Ensure constant adult supervision whenever children are around fireworks.

❌ DON'Ts to Remember

  • 🚫 Never attempt to ignite crackers while holding them in your hands.
  • 🚫 Never bend your face or body directly over fireworks while lighting.
  • 🚫 Never attempt to relight fireworks that did not go off initially (wait 15 mins and pour water).
  • 🚫 Never store fireworks near kitchen stoves, diyas, or open heat sources.
  • 🚫 Never throw ignited crackers towards animals, bystanders, or dry vehicles.
